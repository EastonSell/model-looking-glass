Glaciers form when snow builds up over many years and is pressed into dense ice by its own weight. Once the ice is thick enough, it begins to flow slowly downhill, carving valleys and carrying rock along with it. Most of the fresh water on Earth is stored in glaciers and ice sheets, and their size changes with the climate. During colder periods they advance, and during warmer periods they retreat. Honey bees communicate the location of food through a pattern of movement often called the waggle dance. A returning forager walks in a straight line while shaking her body, then circles back and repeats the run. The direction of the straight part, compared with the vertical surface of the comb, shows the angle of the food relative to the sun. The length of the run shows how far away it is. Other workers follow the dancer closely and then fly out to find the same flowers. Researchers have tested this by moving feeders and watching where new foragers arrive, and the results match the information in the dance remarkably well.

Interest is the price of borrowing money. When you take out a loan, the lender charges a percentage of the amount each year, and that charge is added to what you owe. With simple interest, the charge is always worked out on the original amount. With compound interest, it is worked out on the original amount plus any interest already added, so the debt grows faster the longer it is left. The same idea works in your favour when you save. Light travels more slowly through glass and water than it does through air. When a beam passes from one material into another at an angle, this change of speed makes it bend, an effect known as refraction. Coral reefs cover a tiny part of the ocean floor, yet they are home to about a quarter of all marine species. The reefs are built by small animals called polyps, which take minerals from the water and form hard skeletons. Over thousands of years these skeletons pile up into huge structures. Tiny plants live inside the polyps and give them both food and colour. When the water becomes too warm, the polyps push the plants out and turn white, a process known as bleaching. If the heat does not last long, the coral can recover. If it continues for many weeks, the coral starves, and the fish that depend on it move away.
