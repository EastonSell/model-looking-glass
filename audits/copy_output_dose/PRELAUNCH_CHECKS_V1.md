# Output-dose prelaunch checks

The independent saved-byte reader in this directory is related to the previous prompt-dose reader. Before any new pretrained forward, it replayed two explicitly synthetic fixtures built from the public cases and invented weights. The complete fixture had 288 actions, 80,604,288 action bytes, and an 8/8 positive result in each scheme. Its independent report SHA-256 is `0d85cddad74c660cb1baff97c2cb103c08357572b5f468f359cd1f69e1d4927d`. The second fixture had 252 actions and 69,717,744 action bytes; three families were output-dose ineligible, leaving five evaluable while all eight input-dose families remained evaluable. Its report SHA-256 is `86eb9cf7c573a4bc3eb5e2c31465e7170073adf3f1f496846cdcb9b6ba3bcf00`. A fabricated untouched-coordinate corruption was rejected. These are model-free checks, not study outcomes or checkpoint evidence.

The full retained reports and synthetic raw streams are local. The production reader requires a source-bound manifest, the exact saved journal and result hashes, and the pinned checkpoint; it streams the checkpoint and verifies the retained concatenated projection rows. The separate `checkpoint_binding.py` accepts three split head blocks. Both make byte-consistency claims only. The process observer records the supplied prelaunch commit, direct process and output-file hashes. Its commit argument is caller-supplied, and a spawn return that is unbound remains uncertain; it does not authenticate clocks, interpreter or descendant processes.
