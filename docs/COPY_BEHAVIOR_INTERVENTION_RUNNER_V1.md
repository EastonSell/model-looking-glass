# Descriptive copying-head intervention owner

The [fixed prospective protocol](DISTILGPT2_COPY_BEHAVIOR_SCREEN_V1.md) calls for H2, H10 and same-layer active control H7 on all 12 held-out families whenever their dose plans are valid. The independently replayed [baseline](../results/distilgpt2-copy-behavior-screen-v1-20261029/screen/README.md) supports only five held-out families, below the primary minimum of six. Thus the primary rule is ineligible regardless of these interventions. Supported and unsupported strata, both target rotations and all dose-valid families are retained as descriptive results; no case, head, dose rule or threshold is changed after observing baseline or edit outcomes. This stage-specific code is written after baseline observation, as the protocol disclosed.

The [owner](../scripts/copy_behavior_intervention_v1.py) verifies the original baseline matrix, journal, result and independent replay by their frozen SHA-256 identities. For each fixed family, all eight clean/no-op pairs run first; each clean full-logit row must equal its baseline row bitwise, each no-op must preserve clean logits and its full projection input, and each edit must preserve the untouched coordinates and other-position output. It plans one family-wide projected-output dose from all 24 head/prompt combinations, checks the fixed floor and predicted float32 delivery before editing, runs all preplanned rotated arms and clean-after rows, and then measures actual output norms, residuals and cosines. A finite correctly landed dose mismatch makes that family dose-ineligible; invalid landing, incomplete output or uncertain cleanup invalidates the attempt. Full vocabulary rows, witnesses and projection slices are saved; finite dose mismatches do not cancel later already-planned arms.

Each rotation's mathematical contrast is the repeated target-log-probability change minus the mean of its three shuffled changes. After observing the clean screen and before any intervention forward, the implementation fixed its binary64 evaluation to `fsum((r,r,r,-s1,-s2,-s3))/3`. Each family C and its strict sign use one direct `fsum` over all 24 signed H10/H2 terms before division by six; the stratum mean/sign similarly sums all selected family terms before division. This avoids introducing a false strict-positive sign through separately rounded shuffle means, rotation means or head Ds. The displayed rounded Ds are descriptive and can differ by an ulp from a C evaluated directly from its terms. The terms and sign are retained for independent replay. The threshold itself and the scientific contrast are unchanged.

The E: preflight includes the 154,720,512-byte maximum action payload, 589,824 projection-weight bytes, 355,679,200 verified temporary checkpoint/tokenizer bytes, 32 MiB metadata allowance and 1 MiB failure reserve; C: must have 1 GiB free. The checkpoint is staged explicitly under E: as in the [resource amendment](DISTILGPT2_COPY_BEHAVIOR_SCREEN_RESOURCE_AMENDMENT_V1.md). The bounded manifest, journal, result and failure files do not cap the observer's direct stdout/stderr or all runtime overhead. Disk readings are point-in-time. Source verification and process receipts are cooperative operator observations, not interpreter, clock, external-process or unknown-exposure attestations. Partial returned bytes are retained best-effort on failure; an uncertain spawn or exit is not automatically retried.
