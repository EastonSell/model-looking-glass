# France full-top v2: pre-forward exposure-scope amendment

This amendment modifies the earlier [France v2 protocol](TARGETED_EDIT_FRANCE_FULL_TOP_V2.md),
SHA-256 `c2bc0b6e7932eaa3578d5ad30d74e9665574b55ea307e86c957b3a3cd6910c25`,
before any new study-model forward. Its fixed texts, tokenizer-only manifest,
development/evaluation roles, doses, controls, numerical measurements and
decision thresholds remain unchanged.

The earlier text required a named protected-split check and treated its
unavailability as a stop condition. That requirement is withdrawn. The
study will not query, open, access, compare against, or indirectly obtain
information from any separately protected or flagged split. The absence of
overlap with protected inputs, unknown private history, undocumented uses or
pretraining data is **unverified**; this amendment does not claim otherwise.
The targets are held out from this edit's fitting and calibration, while the
known development and sentinel rows remain exposed as originally documented.

Before the first baseline, compare the fixed study and projection-basis rows
against the then-current, known public inventories and already-fixed pending
public input bundles in both complete-row-prefix directions. Preserve the
specific, documented earlier-study lineage and own-reservation exemptions in
the original protocol. Any other public or pending collision, missing required
public source, or unexplained newer public exposure stops the forward until a
revised, independently reviewed pre-forward protocol and manifest are fixed.
Record source identities, comparison scope, exact exemptions and results;
public-prefix disjointness is not a claim about unobserved inputs.

For a later generated continuation, before each additional model forward,
apply the original bidirectional prefix check to the then-current known
public and fixed pending inputs and this attempt's documented own-reservation
exemption. A public/pending collision or unavailable required public check
stops that forward and leaves the attempt incomplete; no protected or flagged
split is queried for this purpose. Source, capacity, supervision, saved-data
and cleanup requirements in the original protocol remain in force.
