# Paired continuation cases: tokenizer-only scope

The fixed [64-prompt bundle](../experiments/distilgpt2_paired_continuation_cases_v1.json) has SHA-256 `68b567718209285a9adedd588d0c0d269efb4b7d8ed9d278a2fb8201d672d1dd`: 16 seven-token groups and four prompts per group. Every prompt has the same final cue within its family; both candidate continuations are single tokens. The 128 prompt-plus-candidate encodings are score-comparison metadata, not additional model inputs. The saved construction log contains candidate exclusions, selected positions and structural rejections; the first structurally checked combination was selected for each family.

The [fixed protocol](DISTILGPT2_PAIRED_CONTINUATION_PATCH_V1.md) was published before selection. The first construction attempt stopped without cases because some original pools had too few unused token IDs. The prospective [pool amendment](DISTILGPT2_PAIRED_CONTINUATION_POOL_AMENDMENT_V1.md) was published before the new selection and disclosed its tokenizer-only feasibility check; neither attempt loaded a model.

The selector checked the v44 public inventory, its expanded input and continuation forms, the 128/256 public support sets and the then-pending public France bundle SHA-256 `c8dd418bea948c8ca2b4d1e39e696374a28bd2f911bb16707415c38170fbba82` (73 input/extension sequences). An [independent tokenizer-only review](../audits/copy_paired_continuation/case-audit-v1.json) reconstructed all 16 first qualifying groups, all 112 distinct IDs and both candidate extensions and found no relation to 6,815 distinct sequences in those public scopes. A separate public-only comparison checked all 14,016 paired-versus-France sequence pairs and found no bidirectional full-row-prefix relation. Candidate prompts and score extensions are subject to fresh current-inventory and known-pending checks before their reservation and launch. These are public-scope checks, not evidence about protected inputs, undisclosed exposure or model pretraining.
