# Public known-injection localization v1: delivery clarification

Before any pretrained forward on the fixed cases, the delivery check is clarified as follows. In addition to verifying the correctly rounded result and unchanged other bits, compare the saved float32 bits of channel 0 immediately before and after the +4.0 addition. If they are identical, the injection was not delivered in the recorded representation: retain the witness, record an undelivered-injection failure, and stop that attempt as incomplete. Do not count it as a successful localization trial or silently change the injection. An exactly zero downstream logit effect after a bitwise changed injection is instead a valid negative outcome and remains in the fixed analysis. All other protocol rules remain as published in `PUBLIC_KNOWN_INJECTION_LOCALIZATION_V1.md`.
