# Public case construction for the baseline-supported copying screen

The [prospective protocol](DISTILGPT2_COPY_BEHAVIOR_SCREEN_V1.md) fixes the eight vocabulary pools, the split, the two target rotations, the baseline screen, and the intervention rule. The 24 families and 192 tokenizer-only inputs in [the fixed case file](../experiments/distilgpt2_copy_behavior_screen_cases_v1.json) have SHA-256 `96ffdac66a540cef8ff2a64e08649184a7f6fc148b4ef44eb5eedf96b4a18734`. They were assembled without a pretrained forward, and no case was selected or replaced using model outputs. The case file retains all word eligibility decisions, selected positions and attempted-combination rejections. Its construction snapshot is v38, with later public currentness checked separately before reservation and launch.

The selector expands public full inputs, target/candidate continuations, active forms of masked inputs, and scored prefixes and alternatives. Independent [tokenizer-only reconstruction](../audits/copy_behavior_cases_independent_v1.json), with its [reader](../audits/copy_behavior_cases_independent_v1.py), confirmed the selected groups, 144 distinct token IDs, both rotations, every appended target boundary, and no bidirectional prefix relation to the expanded v38 public inputs/support or v39 public inventory. A selector revision broadened its comparisons to the masked and scored forms, and a complete rerun produced the same case bytes. These are public-scope, point-in-time checks; unknown exposures, protected cases and pretraining are outside this conclusion. There is no random sampling or blind selection from earlier published outcomes.
