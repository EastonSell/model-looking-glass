# Private injection result qualification

The saved-evidence qualifier binds the frozen prediction and discovery
runtime to the separately observed held-out runtime, the two prospective
source and runtime departures, the fixed public-case replay plan and its
evidence digest. It checks the exact two recorded runtime manifests and allows
only their disclosed system-environment fingerprint difference. The historical
variable values were not retained, so this comparison does not identify which
values changed. It copies the complete scorer evaluation, including every
site, negative value and undefined restoration, without changing the frozen
selection or assigning a new success threshold. Its output contains private
case details and must remain private until the authorized reveal.

The operation must name the reviewed independent numerical auditor's exact
source digest, and its saved report is checked against the exact evaluation
and journal binding. It must also name the replay producer, claimant, owner
and row-guard source digests in the fixed public replay plan. The qualifier
cannot establish that the auditor or
executor actually exited cleanly, that publication preceded allocation, that
the recorded held-out runtime was used, that the compatibility check passed,
or that a cross-track comparison was authorized. Even a recorded `clear`
receipt is only an identity until separately verified; the journal's receipt
does not establish coverage of every potentially exposed natural successor.
Before evaluation, the final operation must separately bind the exact
qualifier source, and its caller must verify the required comparisons for all
applicable tracks. The returned status
therefore remains `private_saved_evidence_awaiting_execution_attestation`.
The fixed public-case check supports only the tested cases and operations; it
does not establish runtime equivalence or unqualified v1 completion.
