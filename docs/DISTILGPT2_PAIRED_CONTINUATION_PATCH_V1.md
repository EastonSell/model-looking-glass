# Paired continuation patch: prospective protocol

## Question and limits

Can a fixed layer-3 attention-head contribution causally carry evidence about which of two earlier candidate tokens follows a repeated cue, and is that effect larger with the repeated query than with a reordered query ending in the same cue? The preceding baseline-screened study had an ineligible primary comparison and mixed descriptive H2/H10 effects. This design was chosen after those observations; all earlier copying studies are development, not a blind confirmatory set.

The intervention compares H2 with two fixed same-layer active controls, H10 and H7. A positive result would show an influence of the delivered head contribution on candidate odds in these fixed prompts, relative to those controls. It would not establish a unique copying circuit or isolate a single bigram: swapping candidate positions also changes neighboring transitions. The query controls share their final cue and token multiset, but equal projected-output norms do not equal intervention directions, input norms or later nonlinear effects. The words, templates and model are public and the model may have seen related data during pretraining.

Within each pool retain each word's first occurrence, require a single space-plus-word token, and reject IDs already used in an earlier selected family here. In each pool select the first two seven-word combinations in lexicographic pool-position order that satisfy the checks below, re-evaluating exclusions after each family. This gives 16 fixed families and 112 distinct IDs. Record word exclusions and structurally rejected combinations. If any family cannot be constructed, stop; no output may guide a replacement.

For seven distinct tokens `(a,b,c,d,e,f,g)`, form four 12-token prompts:

* `A`: `[a,b,c,d,e,f,g] + [a,b,c,d,e]`;
* `B`: `[a,b,c,d,e,g,f] + [a,b,c,d,e]`;
* `A-order`: `[a,b,c,d,e,f,g] + [b,c,a,d,e]`;
* `B-order`: `[a,b,c,d,e,g,f] + [b,c,a,d,e]`.

The first block has the same token multiset in all four prompts; `e` is the final cue in all of them. The two candidates `f` and `g` are absent from the query suffix and exchange the two positions immediately after the earlier `e`. A and B are paired counterfactuals with an identical query suffix; A-order and B-order are the corresponding fixed reordered-suffix controls. The same final two query tokens remain in both suffixes. Check exact text tokenization and that appending either candidate adds exactly one token without changing the prompt encoding.

Construction uses the reviewed public inventory v44 at commit `4a4078d99d25eba4bbadff16b75e3728524f2c06`, canonical SHA-256 `78d783dc37c1ace4c39300b62e67652d76f3323de79830628ecd64e46aa0bfa0`, the published 128-row and 256-row public supports, and any known fixed pending public bundles. Check every one of the 64 actual prompts and its two scored candidate extensions for full-row prefix relations in both directions against all public input, masked-active, target/candidate/scored-prefix and candidate-extension forms, as well as all other distinct new prompts and their extensions. A prompt and its own two extensions are intentional score metadata and are not compared against one another or counted as model inputs. ## Clean screen and scope

Run all 64 prompts in fixed family/variant order once and retain all 50,257 float32 logits per prompt, including every failure. Define A's correct candidate margin as `logit(f) − logit(g)` and B's as `logit(g) − logit(f)`; each is exactly the two-candidate log-probability odds. A family is **odds-supported** only when both clean margins are at least 1 nat. Independently mark **top-supported** only when the correct candidate is the unique full-vocabulary top in both A and B; a tie is not a unique top. Neither gate is changed or used to replace a family.

The primary population consists of odds-supported families that also pass all dose checks. Report top-supported and non-top families separately, and report all dose-valid families, including odds-unsupported ones, descriptively. For all four clean prompts report both candidate logits, log probabilities, their combined full-vocabulary probability mass, ranks, unique top/tie and each odds margin. The A-order/B-order baseline results are controls and are not another primary gate. A candidate-odds reversal need not be the full-vocabulary top or a generated answer; no multi-token generation claim follows. Missing/nonfinite logits or uncertain execution invalidate the attempt, rather than being counted as unsupported.

## Bidirectional patches and matched doses

For every family, regardless of its clean gate, use each prompt's opposite candidate order with the *same* suffix as its donor: A↔B and A-order↔B-order. First run four no-op witnesses, each requiring full logits bitwise equal to that prompt's clean row and the entire `c_proj` input before and after the no-op to match bitwise. Run four self patches on H2 using the exact same prompt's original final-position head slice; require unchanged full logits, exact landing and complete pre/post projection inputs and projection outputs bitwise equal to that prompt's no-op. All eight captures occur before any active patch for that family.

At layer 3 final input position 11, let `x_A,h` and `x_B,h` be the no-op input slices for head `h` in one suffix condition, and similarly for the reordered condition. With the pinned checkpoint's 64 **input rows** `W_h` of `c_proj` and no bias, compute in binary64 `v = (x_donor,h − x_receiver,h) @ W_h`. Its reverse direction has the opposite unrounded vector. Choose `q = min ||v||` across both suffix conditions and H2/H10/H7. If q is finite and strictly above `2^-8`, use the fixed fraction `q/||v|| ≤ 1` to interpolate each receiver's own slice toward its matched donor. Round each delivered coordinate once to float32; retain the planned and actual bits. No target or edited output selects a dose.

Before the active forwards, require each predicted delivered projected norm to differ from q by at most `10^-4 q` and their 12-way range to be at most `10^-4 q`. Run the 12 active patches in fixed variant-major order A, B, A-order, B-order; within variant rotate H2/H10/H7 left by `(4*(family−1)+variant) mod 3`. Each edited pre-input must equal its receiver's no-op input bitwise, all untouched input coordinates and all other-token-position projection outputs must equal its no-op, and the exact planned head bits must land. Compare each actual final-position `c_proj` output-difference vector with `(landed − receiver_noop) @ W_h`; require its residual norm, its norm error and the 12-way actual norm range each to be at most `10^-4 q`. Save complete pre/post/input/output witnesses, donor-gap input norms, delivered input displacements, fractions, actual output vectors, norms and pairwise direction cosines. After all arms run four clean-after forwards and require their full logits to match the clean rows bitwise.

A finite floor or predicted-dose failure skips the active patches but still runs no-op, self and clean-after controls; its family is dose-ineligible. A finite correctly landed *actual* dose failure marks the family dose-ineligible only after all preplanned arms and controls are retained. Wrong bits, unexplained untouched-position changes, nonfinite values, missing records or uncertain cleanup invalidate the entire attempt. No dose-ineligible family is called a successful or failed primary family.

## Effect and fixed decision

For each receiver/head let the **donorward** change `d` be its clean correct-candidate margin minus its patched correct-candidate margin; positive d shifts two-candidate odds toward the matched donor's candidate. Compute it directly from the four promoted float32 logits; the full-vocabulary normalizer cancels. Decode saved float32 sign, exponent and significand explicitly for top/tie and sign comparisons so ambient denormal-flush state cannot erase subnormal bits. For each head define `S = (d_A + d_B − d_A-order − d_B-order)/2`. Strict signs of d, S and `S_H2 − S_control` are evaluated from the directly combined saved float32 terms before division or displayed rounding, so exact ties are not positives. Retain effects and near-zero values, not just signs.

A primary family meets the fixed H2-versus-controls rule only if `d_A,H2 > 0`, `d_B,H2 > 0`, `S_H2 > 0`, `S_H2 > S_H10` and `S_H2 > S_H7`. With at least six odds-supported and dose-valid families, the criterion is met if at least 75% meet that rule (`4 × successes ≥ 3 × eligible`); otherwise it is criterion-not-met. With fewer than six it is ineligible. This is a prespecified descriptive decision, not a significance test. Report every family and direction, the two candidate masses and full-vocabulary top/ranks, `d`, S, all two-control contrasts, q, input/output norms and cosines, plus full-vocabulary KL(clean‖patch), total variation and top changes. Report the odds-supported/top-supported intersection and all-dose-valid and odds-unsupported strata without changing their denominators or outcomes.
