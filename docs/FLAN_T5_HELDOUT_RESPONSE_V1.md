# FLAN-T5 locally held-out dose-response: prospective CPU protocol

This is a new exploratory follow-up to the public equal-target-length study. That study found four positive and two baseline-unsupported lexical pairs under its fixed operational rule. Here the exact new lexical pairs and templates are withheld locally until the prediction, source, selection recipe and commitments below are fixed. Local withholding limits adaptation to these cases; it does not establish absence from pretraining, a population sample or statistical significance. The earlier studies and their outputs remain unchanged.

## Prediction and source

At the `encoder.final_layer_norm` output of the pinned `google/flan-t5-small` revision `0fc9ddf78a1e988dac52e2dac162b0ede4fd74ab`, predict that the word-position donor interpolation will move the two-target teacher-forced score margin in the donor's direction, grow with dose, and exceed the magnitude of matched instruction- and punctuation-position interpolations. The rule does not bound overshoot beyond the donor's margin. The endpoint and control thresholds below are fixed operational replication criteria, not probabilities, causal exclusivity or a claim that every token conveys no word information. They reuse the published equal-length study's thresholds; that public result informed this follow-up.

Before forming the held-out pool, publish an immutable source commit containing this protocol, the generic runner and tests. Bind the same seven-file asset manifest `d369e3095dc35c52608f40fbd2715d7a2ff80a02fd71f0692c24af2cf3402083` and pinned `T5TokenizerFast` (pad/decoder-start 0, EOS 1). A reviewer independently reads back the source and reports its exact bytes before any selection or held-out model forward. The public record does not prove a trusted time or pretraining independence.

## Withheld pool and model-free selection

A separate case custodian constructs one ordered local pool of exactly 24 distinct pairs of ordinary singular English nouns with one conventional written German target for each noun, and exactly two previously unused templates, without querying the model or consulting scores for the new cases. Each word or target is at most 64 UTF-8 bytes and each template prefix or suffix at most 256 UTF-8 bytes. Each template consists of a fixed prefix and suffix: the source is `prefix + word + suffix`, and its context-only reference is `prefix + suffix` (the removed word is the only edit). A template ends in an ASCII punctuation mark from `.?!:;`; the two templates express structurally different semantic requests, not mere spacing/punctuation variants, and are distinct from the earlier published FLAN templates. Both requests must admit the fixed bare German noun/lemma as a legitimate answer without requiring an article or another inflection. The custodian or an independent sealed reviewer checks the conventional target, ambiguity, inflection and grammatical fit of each selected pair in both contexts before the case commitment. If a selected cell fails that check, retain its reason as `semantic-ineligible`, run no forward for it, never replace it and continue the remaining fixed cells; its lexical pair is unevaluable in each aggregate unless both templates are evaluable. An absent or incomplete review makes the attempt incomplete. The reference may be awkward and must be disclosed, not treated as a natural-language prior. German and English forms are each unique across the pool after Unicode NFC normalization and casefolding and must not reuse any word or target appearing in any prior FLAN-T5 feasibility, matched-dose, dose-response or equal-length case manifest or published candidate screen, including unselected candidates. Record the prior manifests and their immutable revisions in the selection report; do not claim exhaustive knowledge of all model exposure. Candidate order is fixed before tokenization and is not a random sample.

The custodian canonicalizes a JSON payload containing the full ordered pool, both templates, exact protocol hash, immutable source commit and asset hash as UTF-8 with sorted keys, no insignificant whitespace, `ensure_ascii=True` and no nonfinite values. Generate one 32-byte OS-random pool salt and publish/read back the domain-separated SHA-256 commitment `SHA256(b'mlg-flant5-heldout-pool-v1' + bytes([0]) + salt + canonical_pool_bytes)`. Keep the salt and full pool private until the planned reveal. Once this pool commitment is public, never replace, reorder or add candidates and never draw a replacement salt after an uncertain or failed commitment. A commitment binds bytes; the claimed generation order is an operator/reviewer report, not a trusted clock.

Using only the pinned tokenizer and no model, screen **all 24** pool pairs against **both** templates and retain every tokenization and failure reason. Select the first six pairs in the committed order that pass both templates. If fewer than six pass, record `selection-incomplete`, stop and retain the pool/screen; do not replace candidates or revise the rule. Every selected pair has two distinct German targets, each exactly one non-special, non-unknown lexical token covering the complete target plus final EOS; targets and all input attention masks are all ones. Its word and target token IDs must not reuse the respective word or target lexical token IDs in the exact prior case files `FLAN_T5_PUBLIC_CASES_V1.json`, `FLAN_T5_EQUAL_DOSE_CASES_V1.json`, `FLAN_T5_DOSE_RESPONSE_CASES_V1.json`, `FLAN_T5_EQUAL_LENGTH_CASES_V1.json` or full `FLAN_T5_EQUAL_LENGTH_SCREEN_V1.json`. For a prior source word, include every recorded word-position token; for a prior target, include every non-special target token, including all subtokens of rejected candidates. Derive the special IDs from the pinned tokenizer when a prior row lacks a special mask. Bind these files' exact source-commit identities in the published protocol/source and report only eligibility, not hidden text, before reveal. In each template, both source inputs have equal length at most 16 and identical attention and special masks; the context reference also has at most 16 tokens. The only differing token is a single word token whose offset slice stripped of surrounding space equals the full English word. Both sources and their context reference have exactly one final EOS.

Choose the instruction control deterministically as the earliest unchanged attended, non-special, non-word token whose offset lies in the fixed prefix and contains a letter. Choose the punctuation control as the latest unchanged attended, non-special token whose offset is in the fixed suffix and whose text is one of `.?!:;`. Both must exist at the same indices and token IDs for the two sources, and the word, instruction and punctuation indices must be distinct. A prefix token may be a partial written word, and punctuation can carry contextual information. Save texts, IDs, token strings, masks, offsets, chosen indices and eligibility reasons in the private screen and case manifest. Tokenization mismatch, a missing control or an altered identity is not repaired by choosing another site.

Before any held-out forward, bind the complete screen, selected 12 ordered pair/template cells, all 12 sealed semantic verdicts and reasons, pool commitment, source commit, protocol and asset hashes in a second canonical JSON payload. Generate one independent 32-byte OS-random case salt and publish/read back `SHA256(b'mlg-flant5-heldout-cases-v1' + bytes([0]) + case_salt + canonical_payload_bytes)`. Keep case salt, pool salt, pool, screen and cases private through the run. The implementation verifies both commitments and exact source/asset identities before model load, and never redraws a salt, changes a selected case or retries an uncertain run. Both commitments and their publication observations are retained; publication/OS-draw chronology is operator reported, not externally attested.

## Baseline, context reference and eligibility

For each semantically eligible selected cell, score the context reference and both source sentences against both fixed targets without intervention. For target IDs `y`, use decoder IDs `[0] + y[:-1]`, an all-one decoder mask, and `S(y)=math.fsum(float32_log_softmax(logits)[position,target_id])`, including EOS. Define `m_A`, `m_B` and `m_0` as first-target minus second-target score for the two source sentences and the context-only reference, and `Δ=m_A-m_B`. Save every target score and selected-token log probability. The context-only prompt is a descriptive target-prior/context comparator, not a causal correction or an information-free null.

A cell is primary-baseline-supported only when **all** `m_A>0`, `m_B<0`, `Δ>=1.0`, `m_A-m_0>0`, and `m_B-m_0<0` hold. Record each raw and context-reference-relative margin and every failed condition. The extra reference-relative sign requirement is a prospective, stricter context-consistency gate; it can exclude a cell that would have passed the earlier study's raw gate. It neither identifies nor removes lexical priors, and it does not alter an intervention's within-source margin movement because the fixed `m_0` cancels. If any condition fails, the primary status remains `baseline-unsupported`; never replace the cell. Cells with `Δ>=1.0` still run the fixed matched-dose interventions when matchable, even if either sign gate fails: label those results **descriptive secondary** and never count them as primary successes. If `Δ<1.0`, retain the six clean/context forwards and skip patches. Equal target token count does not equalize lexical priors; this score comparison does not establish a greedy translation.

## Matched-dose experiment and fixed outcomes

Use bit-identical target-conditioned clean encoder states. For each site, compute the binary64 source-pair L2 distance `d_s` from its saved float32 vectors and `q=min_s d_s`. If `q/3<=1e-6`, retain `dose-ineligible` and skip patches. Otherwise compute the float32 replacement `own+(f*q/d_s)*(donor-own)` with binary64 intermediates and one final float32 cast, at `f=1/3,2/3,1` for both directions and all three sites. Require each actual displacement positive and within `min(max(1e-5,1e-5*f*q),0.01*f*q)` of the target; in each direction each control displacement must be within 1% of that direction's achieved word displacement. Retain all calculated vectors and `dose-unmatchable` if any check fails; do not run patches for that cell. Matching absolute input L2 does not match perturbation direction, attention weighting or downstream sensitivity; these two control positions do not establish information exclusivity.

For a matched cell, let donor-directed word movement be `w_A(f)=m_A-m_A,word(f)` and `w_B(f)=m_B,word(f)-m_B`, and define `c_instruction` and `c_punctuation` analogously; at zero dose all movements equal zero by definition. A cell is **positive** iff both directions satisfy: (1) word movement is nondecreasing from zero through the three levels allowing `0.0001` nat per adjacent comparison; (2) at `q`, `w>=0.05*Δ`; and (3) at every nonzero fraction, `w>=max(abs(c_instruction),abs(c_punctuation))+0.02*f*Δ`. A supported, matchable cell is **negative** if any inequality fails. Save every signed and absolute movement, all achieved doses, and all applicable `reversed`, `nonmonotone`, `weak_endpoint` and `control_comparison` failure flags. Exact equality passes. For a sign-gate failure, retain the same rule result only as a descriptive secondary status; the primary cell remains `baseline-unsupported`. Model, identity, control or evidence failures make the attempt incomplete, not a negative cell.

Both templates of a lexical pair must be evaluable: both positive make the pair positive, any negative among two evaluable cells makes it negative, and otherwise it is unevaluable. With `P` positive and `N` negative pairs, aggregate positive requires `P>=3 and P>N`, aggregate negative requires `N>=3 and N>P`; all other outcomes are inconclusive. The primary aggregate includes only strict-gate, matchable cells. Separately compute the same both-template pair/aggregate rule as a descriptive raw-gate comparator using cells with `m_A>0`, `m_B<0` and `Δ>=1.0`, regardless of the context-consistency gate, and as a second descriptive Δ-supported aggregate using all matchable cells with `Δ>=1.0`. Report each cell's primary, raw-gate and Δ-supported status/aggregate, raw and strict attrition, all 12 cells and all six pairs. A negative means this thresholded replication rule failed, not that no word effect exists. The two templates share each pair and are not independent lexical examples.

Track precedence is fixed. A `semantic-ineligible` or `alignment-ineligible` cell has that status on all tracks and receives no forward. For a scored cell with `Δ<1.0`, primary and raw-gate statuses are `baseline-unsupported` and the descriptive status is `gap-ineligible`. With `Δ>=1.0`, a failed strict sign gate remains `baseline-unsupported` on the primary track even if the descriptive dose is ineligible or unmatchable; the raw track likewise remains `baseline-unsupported` when its own sign gate fails. For a track whose gate passed, report `dose-ineligible` or `dose-unmatchable` if the common dose fails, otherwise the fixed positive/negative rule. Always record the separate dose/descriptive status and the gate failure reasons.

Use clean, capture-repeat, null, token-self at all three sites, full-state self and donor swap, full natural word donor swap and zero word controls in both directions and for both targets. Require exact CPU full-logit clean/repeat/null/self and full-state-swap/donor equality. Retain and validate same-forward pre/action/post witnesses and untouched positions; verify parameters, buffers, hooks, source and assets before/after. Put the clean capture repeat after all interventions. Natural full word swaps are unmatched-dose descriptive anchors.

## Bounds, custody and reveal

At most 936 root forwards: 12 cells, each with 6 clean/context, 36 anchor/control and 36 matched-dose forwards. Use CPU float32, one thread, deterministic eager attention, offline cache, CUDA hidden, `MKL_CBWR=COMPATIBLE`, `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1`, `MKL_NUM_THREADS=1`, `HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1`, and `TRANSFORMERS_OFFLINE=1`. Start a fresh process with `-B -X pycache_prefix=<fresh-empty-directory>` and check the prefix and source checkout before and after. Require at least 1 GiB free on the output volume and cap the combined retained custody and run evidence at 512 MiB: reserve at most 4 MiB for every pool, screen, cases, salts, cards, observations, copies and bounded failure file, leaving the runner at most 532,676,608 bytes. Check capacity before each custody write and retain the actual complete custody inventory before and after the attempt; an oversized or uncertain inventory is incomplete, never truncated or replaced. Bound every candidate/template, pool/case payload and observation before serialization/reading. With source length at most 16, the conservative 936-return run allowance is 363,257,856 tensor bytes; 122,683,392 bytes for two capped 64 KiB JSON records per forward; 737,280 dose-vector bytes; 2,752,512 bytes for bounded case/dose/startup/terminal JSON; and 1,421,312 bytes for observation and one malformed-return/failure allowance. The sum is 490,852,352 bytes, plus a 1,486,848-byte next-forward reserve, below the runner allowance. Per-file JSON bounds and both allowances must be enforced; a cap failure is incomplete. The allowance is not reserved disk blocks or a transient-memory bound. The previous 648-forward CPU run retained 185,522,872 bytes and peaked at 1,016,471,552 working-set bytes; these are observations, not promises for this run. A GPU path is separate and does not gate the CPU attempt.

Use one fresh protected local evidence directory, preserve the original pool, both salts/commitments and all partial/failure files, and do not run a second held-out attempt if exit or completion is uncertain. Freeze and publish a saved-byte auditor tested on fabricated cases before any held-out forward; keep per-cell/aggregate logs and real output private from the auditor author until that freeze. A separate reviewer may then inspect the closed run and sealed data. After the result and audit are fixed, reveal the committed pool, selection, salts, cases and complete retained public evidence so another reader can recompute both commitments and all scores. If selection is incomplete or the attempt fails, publish the failure status and retain and reveal the committed pool, available screen, salts and bounded failure evidence after the decision to stop; no replacement attempt is permitted. Record the process exit and the author-reported freeze/inspection chronology with their limits. Do not infer causal word localization, cross-population generalization, or why prior cells were unsupported from these examples.

Launch the source-bound CPU owner itself with `-B -X pycache_prefix=<custody>/owner-parent-bytecode-prefix`, where that path is initially nonexistent; it starts the runner once with its own separate fresh bytecode prefix. It buffers each private stdout/stderr stream up to 64 KiB. The owner retains both streams, the observed child exit, both bytecode-prefix checks and a final self-inclusive custody inventory inside the 4 MiB share. A stream exceeding its cap makes the attempt incomplete and its unretained suffix is disclosed; it is not counted as a negative result. A generous one-hour process deadline and bounded terminate/kill waits make a timeout incomplete, with any unconfirmed child exit reported rather than assumed. The recorded exit and inventories are local operator observations, not independent proof of execution or a trusted timestamp. Any failure before the runner creates its evidence directory is likewise retained as a bounded private owner record where storage permits.
