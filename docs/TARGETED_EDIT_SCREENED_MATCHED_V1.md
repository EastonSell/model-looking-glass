# Screened projected-edit follow-on: prospective protocol

**Status: protocol only.** No new pool cases have been assembled or forwarded under this protocol. Publish this protocol before assembling them. Freeze and publish the case inventory, source and runtime identities before their first model forward; freeze the edit construction and discovery-only dose match before observing any new target-screen scores. Preserve the prior projected study's ineligible result unchanged.

This small CPU study asks two distinct questions: how a locality-projected rank-one edit compares with the unprojected direction at the same parameter norm, and how they compare when the unprojected dose approximately matches the projected edit's change on one old discovery prompt. The second match is on that discovery prompt only. It is not a guarantee of matched target efficacy on new prompts. Paris is the capital of France, so raising London over Paris is a deliberate counterfactual probe. Both comparisons are exploratory; a next-token margin is not generated text, a stored belief, or a causal-localization result.

The [previous projected study](../results/targeted-edit-projected-cpu-v1-20261028/README.md) was ineligible because only two of four target margins were negative at baseline. Its two government-location targets were not strict capital paraphrases. This follow-on uses direct-capital target cases and a baseline-only screen. The earlier public result and its development measurements informed this design, including the dose grid below; neither the design nor its screen is independent of researchers' prior knowledge. New selected cases are held out from constructing the edit, but their baseline scores are observed and used for selection. They are public, non-blind, baseline-conditioned, and not a representative sample. Unknown pretraining and prior exposure remain unknown.

## Model and edit construction

Use the same pinned DistilGPT2 checkpoint, CPU eager float32 execution, single intra-op and inter-op threads, offline assets, deterministic and denormal settings, full state and registered-buffer checks, and final-block MLP projection site as the previous projected protocol. Bind exact source, tokenizer and checkpoint hashes, runtime settings, and the executed public input IDs in a published manifest. Validate every target prompt plus each London and Paris continuation and every locality prompt plus its expected continuation against the pinned tokenizer; all continuations must append as the expected single token without retokenizing the prompt. Finite logits, exact row counts and smallest-token-ID argmax ties are required.

Use only the already-public v1 discovery prompt and the same four already-public calibration prompts to construct the edit. The target is the final-position London-minus-Paris logit margin. Before construction, save and require byte-identical full discovery-site inputs, outputs and logits between original gradient-enabled and no-grad forwards. Capture the uncentered original-model site inputs at every token position of all four calibration prompts; require discovery and calibration site inputs to match their original bytes under each edit. In CPU float64 use torch.linalg.svd with full_matrices=False, retaining right singular vectors with singular value strictly greater than 1e-10 times the largest. Let P project off their span. Require finite nonzero norms, positive largest singular value and retained rank, ||P h||/||h|| at least 1e-6, ||X(P h)||/(||X||F ||h||) at most 1e-8, and ||G - h g^T||F / ||G||F at most 1e-5. Invalid or collapsed factors end the attempt; do not redraw or change a cutoff. Record singular values, rank, retained and discarded input-factor fractions, full gradient and rank-one residual, all factors, and planned, intended and realized gradient dot products. For each edit, record the actual before/after site outputs for all 18 calibration tokens, the separate per-token algebraic X times realized-delta effects, and the full final-position logit rows for each of the four calibration prompts. The calibration null is a construction check on those same calibration inputs, not evidence of locality on new inputs. The saved full-gradient dot is the derivative of the gradient-enabled execution; matched forward values do not by themselves prove matching downstream Jacobians.

The projected and unprojected gradient edits each use a planned Frobenius norm of 0.001 times the original site weight norm. Form and normalize each non-opposite outer product in float64, cast the intended delta once to float32 and add it to the original float32 weight. Save the full gradient, factors and scale information, plus norms and SHA-256 digests of the original weight, each float64 planned direction, float32 intended delta, float32 edited weight and float64 post-addition realized delta, using contiguous little-endian bytes. Do not publish the raw original or edited checkpoint weights; independently reconstructing realized deltas requires the pinned checkpoint. Require each realized norm finite and nonzero and within [0.999, 1.001] of its planned norm; all equal-norm controls must also match the projected realized norm within that ratio. Use one fresh projected rank-one Gaussian random control: a dedicated CPU torch.Generator with seed 20261103 draws a float64 length-3072 factor followed by a float64 length-768 factor with no intervening draw; project the first factor with P. Use a sign reversal of the projected intended float32 delta. For one fresh rank-matched nuisance-span control, a separate CPU torch.Generator with seed 20261104 makes one float64 torch.randn((3072, rank)) draw; take its reduced torch.linalg.qr span, project h off it and pair with g. Require finite QR results, each absolute R diagonal strictly greater than 1e-12 times the largest, and each projected factor to retain at least 1e-6 of its original norm. The two random draws are fixed, separate, single descriptive controls; do not redraw or select them from results. Record the Torch version and CPU build.

## Discovery-only effect match

Before looking at any new target-screen outputs, measure original and projected discovery logits and the actual unprojected discovery result for every planned norm fraction in this fixed ascending grid: 0.0005, 0.000625, 0.00075, 0.000875, 0.001, 0.001125 and 0.00125. Each candidate rescales the same frozen unprojected direction, with its own intended and realized post-addition hashes and planned/realized norm check. Use the same restored original model and full-state, buffer and denormal checks for each condition. Save the full discovery rows, actual gains, all candidate norms and failures. Do not assume the first-order prediction is the measured change.

Let gP and gU(d) be the actual float64 differences of the saved discovery London-minus-Paris margins from the same original baseline. A match is available only when gP > 0.25 and at least one gU(d) > 0.25. Among candidates with gU(d) > 0.25, select the smallest absolute discrepancy |gU(d) - gP|; resolve exact ties by the lower grid dose. Require that discrepancy to be at most max(0.05, 0.10 times |gP|). If it fails after a complete valid grid, record **discovery match unavailable**, serialize the selected dose as null, and do not add or interpolate grid points. Record the nearest candidate with gU(d) > 0.25 and its discrepancy separately, or null when no candidate meets that floor; retain every grid result and the specific reason for unavailability. A failed or ambiguous grid forward, restoration or cleanup is invalid/incomplete, not a normal no-match result. Seal and publish the selected dose, every candidate measurement, the projected and unprojected intended/realized update identities, all control identities and the discovery-only record before the first new target-screen forward. An unavailable match makes only the matched-discovery arm ineligible; the equal-norm arm may still run. If the selected grid point is 0.001, require its intended-delta and realized-weight hashes to equal the equal-norm unprojected hashes and reuse that exact unprojected condition and saved outputs. Label both comparisons as sharing this arm, not as independent replications.

The grid was chosen after the earlier development outcome was public. In that earlier run the 0.1% projected edit increased the discovery margin by about 1.175 but did not reverse its negative sign. Do not use a discovery-reversal gate or present this known value as a new blind test.

## Cases and baseline-only screen

After this protocol is published, assemble a fixed pool of exactly 32 distinct public target prompts in four documented direct-capital wording strata, eight per stratum, and 12 fixed public locality prompts. Each target must unambiguously ask for France's national capital and support either single-token London or Paris as its immediate continuation. Do not substitute a question about the seat or location of government. A reviewer must check each target's meaning before any new model forward. Freeze IDs, exact text, token rows, continuations, stratum labels and within-stratum order; do not change or replenish the pool after it is frozen.

Locality has four other-country capital prompts, four non-capital France-related prompts and four unrelated facts. Include at least one semantically appropriate locality whose expected continuation is London and one whose expected continuation is Paris; neither may ask the counterfactual France-capital question. Keep every locality row regardless of its original top token or expected-token correctness. Disclose the strata and report each separately; they are small probes, not a capability sample.

Freeze and tokenizer-validate all 76 prompt-plus-continuation encodings (London and Paris for each target, one expected continuation for each locality) as continuation metadata, not as 76 additional model forwards. Before freezing the 44 actual input prompts and again before each model-input stage, compare their prompt token rows against the then-current reviewed public exposure inventory, every other new prompt and each relevant protected-split custodian using exact and both complete-row-prefix directions. For a custodian with a protected split, require its private pass/fail comparison without inspecting private rows. A scoped report that no protected rows yet exist is a distinct observation and does not claim a private comparison occurred. Stop on a collision, unavailable check, stale inventory or unknown custody. The 13 prior projected evaluation cases are not candidates and are never relabeled as held out; reject exact/prefix relationships with those and all other known rows. Reserve the 44 prompt input rows before their first forward. On later checks, exempt only this study's exact, manifest-bound reservation identities and token rows and the authorized same-attempt screen exposures, while continuing to check every other record, including independently duplicated rows. No check certifies unknown history.

After the discovery record is frozen, run the original model on all 32 target candidates once, in frozen order, without applying any edit. Save all full-vocabulary float32 logit rows, ordered hashes and every London-minus-Paris baseline margin. A row is eligible exactly when its finite margin is strictly less than zero; zero and positive margins are not eligible. Select up to eight eligible rows deterministically as follows: begin with stratum 1 and visit strata 1, 2, 3, 4 repeatedly; at each visit take the next eligible unused row in that stratum's frozen order, skipping exhausted strata. Stop when eight rows have been chosen or all strata are exhausted. If fewer than eight total rows qualify, both formal arms are **ineligible**; nevertheless evaluate the 0–7 selected eligible rows and all 12 fixed localities descriptively under the same already-frozen conditions. Never add baseline-positive fillers, replace cases or relax a rule. When zero targets qualify, target aggregates, reversal rates and the evaluated-target matching comparison are null/not evaluable, not zero; for 1–7 report per-row and descriptive target metrics but do not apply the eight-target success rule. Publish all 32 scores, each eligibility decision, the selected IDs and counts by stratum, and a complete hash-bound selection record before the first edited forward on any new case. Selection uses baseline sign and order only: never use edited outputs, locality outcomes, margin magnitude ranking or a replacement pool.

## Evaluation and reporting

Evaluate the selected 0–8 targets and all 12 frozen locality rows under the original model, projected gradient, equal-norm unprojected gradient, projected random, opposite projected, and nuisance-span conditions. If a discovery-effect match exists, add the selected unprojected-dose condition. Reuse the exact committed screening logits as the original target rows, verifying selected IDs, offsets, lengths and byte hashes before any edited new-case forward; do not silently replace them with another baseline. The original locality rows come from the pinned original model in the evaluation stage. Restore and check the original full state, site weight, parameters, registered buffers, hooks and gradients before and after every condition. Check denormal mode before and after each weight addition and score calculation. Bind each intended and realized edit to the pre-screen record, preserve failed/cancelled attempts and independently verify owner exit and cleanup.

For every condition and every row, save full-vocabulary logits and row hashes; use shifted float64 log-softmax to compute London-minus-Paris margins, both candidate NLLs, expected-locality NLL, KL(original || condition) and full-vocabulary top ID. Report per-case values and changes, all condition aggregates, and the three locality-stratum aggregates. For an eligible eight-target arm, target means and reversal counts use all eight selected negative-baseline targets; a reversal means the edited margin is strictly positive. Report the actual reversal count and exact evaluated-mean difference even if the approximate match criterion passes. Report both the mean and every individual NLL change, including baseline-wrong locality cases, so compensating gains and losses cannot hide a deterioration. Also report selected-stratum counts and every excluded screen row.

For the equal-norm arm, let LP and LU be the mean KL over all 12 locality rows under projected and equal-norm unprojected edits. Its frozen **success** rule requires all of: at least two target reversals; mean projected target gain strictly greater than 0.25; mean projected target gain exceeds the projected-random mean by at least 0.10; every locality top ID unchanged; mean locality KL at most 0.02, maximum locality KL at most 0.10, mean expected-token NLL deterioration at most 0.05 and maximum individual NLL deterioration at most 0.20 nats; LP at most 0.9 times LU; and LU - LP at least 1e-6. Otherwise, if eligible and complete, it is **negative**. These thresholds are exploratory design choices made after the preceding public study, not statistical significance thresholds.

For the matched-discovery arm, use the identical projected results and compare with the fixed selected unprojected dose. In addition to the same projected target/random and locality safeguards, require the selected unprojected mean target gain to exceed 0.25, the absolute difference between the two evaluated mean target gains to be at most max(0.05, 0.25 times the larger absolute mean gain), and the projected mean locality KL to meet the same 10% and 1e-6 advantages relative to the matched unprojected mean KL. All must pass for **success**; otherwise an available, complete arm is **negative**. The evaluated-target tolerance is a predeclared outcome criterion, never a reason to retune the matched dose. If the discovery match is unavailable, the matched arm is **ineligible**, and the equal-norm result is still reported.

Execution, source/custody, incomplete-output or unverified-cleanup failures take precedence as **invalid/incomplete**. Otherwise insufficient eligible screen rows make both arms **ineligible**; an unavailable discovery match makes only the matched arm ineligible; eligible complete arms are success or negative by their fixed criteria. Report all raw criteria even on failure. Do not infer significance, broad preservation, or unique attribution to the calibration span from a single public prompt family and single random/nuisance draws. The earlier result is not reclassified and binary labels from different case-selection rules or doses are not directly comparable.

The independent saved-output reader must recompute the screen selection, condition metrics and terminal precedence from the published bytes; a separate reviewer checks the mathematics and case meanings. Execution, clock ordering, cleanup and source/custody records are cooperative attestations, not authenticated proof or protection against hostile concurrent mutation. This study is motivated by the distinction between specificity and generalization in [ROME](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.05262) and by the caution that localization need not identify the best edit site in [Hase et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.04213); it does not claim to reproduce either method's conclusions.
