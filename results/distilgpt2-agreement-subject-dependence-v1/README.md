# Agreement subject-dependence prelaunch record

This record binds the 80 public, nonblind inputs and the planned one-shot CPU
attempt of up to 720 forwards in the
[prospective protocol](../../docs/AGREEMENT_SUBJECT_DEPENDENCE_V1.md).
The fixed L3 direction was derived from previously exposed examples. The
follow-up compares the same signed intervention across matched subject forms
and separately measures fragments; it does not by itself establish an
abstract grammatical-number feature. At the original prelaunch publication,
no subject-dependence study forward had been run.

The prelaunch record fixes the case, protocol, direction and output-reference
bytes, source revision, checkpoint, CPU runtime, exposure reservation and
scoped custodian observations. The child reconstructs the source-bound
inventory recorded in the prelaunch; any newer current inventory is a
separately identified operator observation, checked for preservation and
known-prefix conflicts. Neither check proves that the model's historical
exposure is complete.

canonical bytes are available as `exposure-v27.canonical.json.gz`; the
uncompressed SHA-256 is
`ef42683a664dda450e82c69afedda34a8a34f2daf9f50f2db54f6c9d846f07e5`.

[agreement-direction observer](../distilgpt2-agreement-direction-study-v1/gated_observer.py)

`pending-inputs-comparison.json` reports no full-row-prefix relations. This is
a point-in-time public-input comparison, not a child-verified claim.

The natural public-support receipt compares the fixed 80 inputs against the
128-row reconstructed original support and the 256-row successor pool in

without a scientific result after a possible-forward announcement. The

these scopes and do not authenticate them or rule out unknown exposures.

The operator checks the published observer and support hashes, isolated base
interpreter, clean exact owner source, current reviewed exposure and both
custodians before the one-shot launch. The unchanged reviewed rooted gated
observer compares the supervised direct child's run ID and PID with the child
custody record. Treating a `complete_candidate` as a study result also requires

drain. Output is inherited, and physical file accounting is a cooperative
post-run check rather than an operating-system quota. An incomplete or
uncertain attempt must be preserved and not retried.

cache directory. At launch the operator checks at least 2 GiB free on the

share a volume. These are point-in-time checks, not reserved capacity; RAM,
filesystem allocation and other process or external-copy use are excluded.
