# FLAN-T5 matched-dose encoder intervention: CPU result

The fixed exploratory rule was **negative**: the matched word-position interventions shifted the target-score margin toward the donor and exceeded both matched-position controls, but failed the separate 20%-of-baseline-gap requirement in at least one direction for every evaluable cell. Both apple/orange cells were baseline-unsupported and were not patched. These are results for public, correlated examples, not a statistical test or evidence of generalization.

## Question and decision rule

The study used the pinned Apache-2.0 `google/flan-t5-small` revision `0fc9ddf78a1e988dac52e2dac162b0ede4fd74ab`, with an intervention at `encoder.final_layer_norm` on full CPU model forwards. For each source pair and template, the score is the complete teacher-forced log probability of the fixed first German target minus that of the second. Each target includes EOS; these sums are sensitive to target length and prior frequency and are not generated translations.

Before any patch, the protocol required the clean margin to be positive for the first source, negative for the second and separated by at least one nat. For supported cells, it set the same absolute L2 dose `q` for the word position and the instruction and punctuation control positions, interpolating each receiver toward its paired donor without extrapolation. A cell was positive only if **both** directional word movements were at least 20% of the clean margin gap and exceeded the absolute movement at each matched control position by at least 5% of that gap. A lexical pair was positive if both templates were positive, negative if both were evaluable and at least one was negative, and otherwise unevaluable. The aggregate required at least two positive or two negative lexical pairs; otherwise it was inconclusive. These thresholds were fixed exploratory rules, not significance thresholds.

The [protocol at the source used for the run](https://github.com/EastonSell/model-looking-glass/blob/6f74b7506b577154b0ed0b42eb3b7db8a5e3ed70/docs/FLAN_T5_EQUAL_DOSE_V1.md) and [fixed public cases and tokenization](https://github.com/EastonSell/model-looking-glass/blob/6f74b7506b577154b0ed0b42eb3b7db8a5e3ed70/docs/FLAN_T5_EQUAL_DOSE_CASES_V1.json) were published at commit `6f74b7506b577154b0ed0b42eb3b7db8a5e3ed70` before the operator's recorded run. The public-source observation is saved in the original evidence directory. The observation and timestamps are operator/reviewer records, not independently trusted proof of chronology or execution.

## Results

For each eligible cell, the table shows the signed donor-directed word movement divided by the clean margin gap, first-source and second-source directions respectively. The last numeric column is the larger absolute matched-control movement divided by that gap, for each corresponding direction. The raw margins, scores, positions, doses, control movements and decisions are in the case and forward records.

| Pair | Template | Common L2 dose `q` | Word movement / gap | Largest control movement / gap | Cell |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| apple / orange | translate | — | — | — | baseline unsupported |
| apple / orange | question | — | — | — | baseline unsupported |
| water / fire | translate | 0.35662 | 0.1720 / 0.1706 | 0.0080 / 0.0030 | negative |
| water / fire | question | 0.23255 | 0.0946 / 0.0850 | 0.0086 / 0.0032 | negative |
| bird / fish | translate | 0.30222 | 0.2071 / 0.1854 | 0.0071 / 0.0204 | negative |
| bird / fish | question | 0.16147 | 0.1066 / 0.0743 | 0.0056 / 0.0060 | negative |

All four evaluable cells satisfied the 5%-gap comparison against **both** controls in both directions. Each failed the independent 20%-gap condition in at least one direction; the bird/fish translate cell met it in one direction only. Thus the negative decision is not a claim of zero word-position effect. The apple/orange clean margins were −2.2352 / −9.0052 for translate and −0.4141 / −6.3758 for question, so the first-source preference was not positive in either template. That is an unsupported baseline for these fixed teacher-forced scores, not proof that the model cannot translate those words.

On the four evaluable cells, the saved captured-repeat, null, token-self and full-state-self logits match the clean references bit-for-bit; complete-state swap logits match the donor reference. The same-forward witnesses show the selected patch landing and untouched encoder positions. The observed common doses range from 0.16147 to 0.35662, or 6.38% to 17.29% of the corresponding clean receiver-vector norm. Matching input-vector L2 does not match intervention direction, downstream functional dose or contextual information at each position, so this comparison alone does not establish a unique word-localized translation mechanism.

## Evidence and independent checks

The original closed run contains 228 model forwards and 66,272,429 retained bytes, including full little-endian float32 logits, encoder states, same-forward witnesses, dose vectors and metadata. `run/completed.json` has SHA-256 `8d0d6e166fd8ced06b5066f7ccb6e27e0a12c97124bffb47e57ca304600a51d6`. An [operator observation](operator-observation.json) reports child exit 0 and binds the started and completed records; it does not independently attest execution or provide a trusted timestamp. The saved environment records CPU float32, one thread, deterministic execution, eager attention and `MKL_CBWR=COMPATIBLE`; the runner requires offline flags before proceeding. Its observed peak process working set was 1,033,588,736 bytes, an observation rather than a memory bound or GPU measurement.

The [full saved-byte replay](audit/full-saved-replay.json) checked all 228 records, exact float32 selected log probabilities, same-forward landing, unchanged positions, self/null/full-swap logits, dose construction and decisions. Its [auditor source](audit/full-saved-replay.py) was [published](https://github.com/EastonSell/model-looking-glass/commit/ceb416d5687ed0d60b9ced8a296eed11f0cd3e76) after the run exited and before its author inspected the real result. A [second replay](audit/full-saved-replay-localization.json) parsed identically. The independent [decision and landing check](audit/decision-and-landing.json) verified the stored score arithmetic, hashes, controls and aggregate without independently reconstructing log-softmax; its preserved [script](audit/decision-and-landing.py) uses workspace-relative paths and is included as the exact reviewed artifact. The separate standard-library [scalar replay](audit/scalar-replay.json) checked hashes, EOS shifts, all rounded dose deliveries and decisions, and compared every saved selected float32 token score with a binary64 log-sum-exp calculation. Its [source](audit/scalar-replay.py) was fixed before inspecting real evidence. The largest per-token difference was `1.6938748802264314e-05`, below the predeclared diagnostic tolerance `0.001`; decisions use the saved float32 scores. None of these saved-evidence audits proves that the model actually executed or that the public examples are unseen in pretraining.

The [publication index](publication-index.json) gives the size and SHA-256 of each included file other than the index itself. The raw records are kept individually under `run/`; no model weights are redistributed here.
