# Answer-format feasibility on DistilGPT2

The prespecified format outcome is **ineligible**: neither development family had three facts whose factual city was the unique full-vocabulary top token under all four fixed prefixes. The selection was therefore null. As required, all 32 held-out rows were still run and retained; neither held-out family had any fact that was robust across all four prefixes. No edit target was selected and no edit was made. The fixed robustness criterion was not met on these four facts and templates; it does not rule out every answer format or edit.

The experiment also found a strong, separately descriptive response to the two fixed examples. On held-out rows, the Spain–Madrid condition had ` Madrid` as unique top in 7/8 cases, while the Sweden–Stockholm condition had ` Stockholm` as unique top in 8/8. The predeclared D contrast is the change in Madrid-versus-Stockholm log odds between those two conditions. Averaged over both held-out templates, its value was positive for all four facts: France +15.9193, Japan +15.5427, Italy +15.4213 and United Kingdom +17.3211 nats. This meets the fixed 3-of-4 descriptive-direction rule. Both country and city text change between the two demonstrations, so these results show a response to these particular contexts; they do not identify a copying mechanism. The held-out templates reuse the four development facts, and the study provides no population or significance claim. A next-token top is not a complete generated answer.

All eight held-out factual tops were question-family rows: four without a prefix and four with the neutral prefix; no held-out statement row had a factual top. All 64 vocabulary maxima were unique. The neutral and example prefixes each have eight tokenizer tokens, while no prefix is eight tokens shorter; comparisons to the latter can include position or length effects. The neutral and exemplar contexts also differ in semantic content. The fixed seeds randomize execution order only; they do not turn four facts or their correlated templates into independent population samples. The previous full-top study supplied discovery history and is not held-out evidence.
