# Public two-attractor activation-patching follow-up

This small prospective test asks how a previously selected DistilGPT-2 attention-output site responds when one or both attractor nouns are changed. It uses 32 fixed public inputs in eight subject/number/order units and compares the selected whole-module output at `transformer.h.3.attn`, position 7, with the earlier `transformer.h.1.attn` site. The 112 planned CPU forwards completed. The 32 cases and site choices were fixed before these cases were forwarded; the separate 128-case behavioral screen's outcomes were available to the protocol author before the final protocol was published, so this follow-up was not blind to that screen. The site was selected by earlier public discovery, not by these 32 inputs.

The contrast uses the subject-signed `is − are` logit margin. `I_clean = M11 − M10 − M01 + M00` compares clean inputs; `I_patch = δ11 − δ10 − δ01` compares replacing the all-congruent recipient's site vector with near-only, beside-only, or both-mismatch donors. These are descriptive logits; no success, significance, or equivalence threshold was specified.

| Subject number | Units | Mean clean interaction | L1 mean patch interaction | L3 mean patch interaction |

| Singular | 4 | +0.11504 | −0.00408 | +0.01216 |
| Plural | 4 | −0.11403 | −0.02491 | +0.02405 |
| Both | 8 | +0.00051 | −0.01450 | +0.01811 |

The near-zero pooled clean mean conceals opposite subject-specific means. At L3 the mean near, beside, and both patch shifts are −0.17172, −0.02940, and −0.18302; at L1 they are −0.04974, −0.04741, and −0.11164. The L3 beside shift averages +0.07332 for singular subjects and −0.13212 for plural subjects. Individual units, all four clean margins, patch shifts, past-tense results, group summaries, and ties are retained in `result.json` and the audit.

All 32 clean cases and all 24 donor-patched targets at each site favored the subject-correct `is/are` and `was/were` alternative, with no ties. This is pairwise accuracy, not a full-vocabulary top-token claim. All same-input no-op and self-patch selected logits matched the clean recipient bitwise. All donor landing, selected-slice consistency, target, and untouched-position checks passed.

The mean L2 donor displacements for near/beside/both were 2.623/3.193/4.428 at L1 and 2.969/5.196/5.372 at L3. These are unequal interventions. Position 7 is the beside noun itself, so beside and both donors also replace a locally different lexical token; whole-vector replacement transfers other contextual differences, and the downstream readout is nonlinear. The results do not establish independent attractor channels, additivity, or a unique grammatical mechanism. This single-template, eight-unit study also cannot establish pretraining novelty or population-level significance.
