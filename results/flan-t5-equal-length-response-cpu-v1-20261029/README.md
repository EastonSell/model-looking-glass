# FLAN-T5 equal-length matched-dose response: CPU result

The fixed exploratory rule was **positive** on four lexical pairs: man/woman, father/mother, day/night and car/train met the cell criteria in both templates. Chair/table and coffee/tea were baseline-unsupported in both templates and received no intervention. Thus four pairs were positive, none negative and two unevaluable, satisfying the prespecified aggregate rule. The 648-forward result applies to these fixed public examples, the chosen layer, directions, doses and controls; it is not a statistical significance test or evidence of broad generalization.

## Question and fixed rule

The [protocol](https://github.com/EastonSell/model-looking-glass/blob/9d9aa69f208f8152db9c2832e0fc4ed63ef16df4/docs/FLAN_T5_EQUAL_LENGTH_RESPONSE_V1.md), [complete tokenizer-only screen](https://github.com/EastonSell/model-looking-glass/blob/9d9aa69f208f8152db9c2832e0fc4ed63ef16df4/docs/FLAN_T5_EQUAL_LENGTH_SCREEN_V1.json), [selection plan](https://github.com/EastonSell/model-looking-glass/blob/9d9aa69f208f8152db9c2832e0fc4ed63ef16df4/docs/FLAN_T5_EQUAL_LENGTH_SELECTION_V1.md) and [fixed cases](https://github.com/EastonSell/model-looking-glass/blob/9d9aa69f208f8152db9c2832e0fc4ed63ef16df4/docs/FLAN_T5_EQUAL_LENGTH_CASES_V1.json) were published at the independently observed source commit before the operator's recorded run. The six pairs were the first eligible entries of a 16-pair list using tokenizer properties alone; each German target has one lexical token plus EOS. The public examples are not blind or known to be absent from pretraining.

The score margin is the complete teacher-forced log probability of the first fixed German target minus the second, including EOS. A cell was eligible for interventions only when the first-source margin was positive, the second-source margin negative and their difference `Δ` at least one nat. Otherwise it retained six clean/context forwards and was skipped. For a supported cell, `q` is the minimum natural donor/receiver L2 distance at the word, instruction and punctuation positions. Each site was interpolated toward its paired donor without extrapolation at `q/3`, `2q/3` and `q`, and the actual float32 displacement was checked to match within the fixed 1% limits.

A cell was positive only if, in **both** directions, donor-directed word movement was nondecreasing from the zero-dose baseline through the three levels with a `0.0001`-nat allowance, reached at least `0.05Δ` at `q`, and exceeded the larger absolute control movement by at least `0.02 × dose fraction × Δ` at every nonzero dose. Both templates had to be evaluable for a pair; both positive made it positive, while an evaluable negative template made the pair negative. Aggregate positive required at least three positive pairs and more positive than negative evaluable pairs; ties or too few evaluable pairs were inconclusive. These are fixed operational thresholds, not calibrated significance thresholds.

At `q`, only one of the 16 supported receiver directions crossed the original two-target margin's zero point: the first-source man/woman translate margin moved from `0.50493` to `−0.62741`. The other 15 retained their original margin sign. The descriptive full natural word-state swaps reversed the two-target margin in all 16 directions, but used a larger, unmatched dose: `q` was 8.96%–27.03% of the natural word-state distance across these cells. The rule tests directional shifts, not sign reversal or a generated translation. The two templates reuse each lexical pair and are not independent lexical examples.

The two unsupported pairs were not replaced. For chair/table, the first-source margins were negative in both templates; for coffee/tea, the second-source margins were positive in both templates. All four gaps exceeded one nat, but each failed its corresponding absolute-sign condition. The context-only question omits the question mark as well as the word and is a descriptive reference, not a matched no-word counterfactual. Equal target-token counts do not equalize lexical priors or establish why the preceding three-pair dose-response attempt was unsupported. Matched input L2 does not equalize direction, functional effect or information at the control positions; the result does not show that translation information is exclusive to the word position. English `train` is ambiguous; the fixed target was the noun `Zug`.

## Evidence and reproduction

The original run retained 1,976 files and 185,522,872 apparent bytes, including all raw float32 logits, encoder states, same-forward witnesses, dose vectors, control records and outcomes. The [source index](source-index.json) binds every original file and its lossless ZIP part. `python extract-and-verify.py --verify-only` checks the part and member SHA-256s without extraction; replace `--verify-only` with a fresh output directory to reconstruct the original `run/` directory without overwriting existing files. Model weights are not included. The [publication index](publication-index.json) separately binds every published file except itself.

The terminal `run/completed.json` SHA-256 is `6e4caed8a4b5372c09d30e3140a3ce50dadf1b822dbcb69df40e8efd1d6713a8`. The [operator observation](operator-observation.json) reports exit zero, a clean checkout and an empty fresh bytecode prefix. The run recorded CPU float32, one thread, deterministic eager attention and `MKL_CBWR=COMPATIBLE`; the runner required the offline flags. The public-source and process observations are reports, not independent proof of execution or trusted clocks.

Saved-byte replays checked all 648 raw records, EOS-inclusive scores, rounded dose construction, same-forward landing, untouched positions, self/null/full-state controls, curve decisions and unsupported cells. The [full replay](audit/full-saved-replay.json) uses the separately published [auditor](audit/full-saved-replay.py); its author reports freezing the script after execution and before inspecting this output. A [second replay](audit/related-saved-replay.json) using a related [probe](audit/related-saved-replay.py) reached the same result; it shares substantial structure with the first auditor and requires the original reviewed source checkout. A separately implemented [scalar replay](audit/scalar-replay.json) with its [source](audit/scalar-replay.py) checked the scores, dose bits and decisions using an independent binary64 diagnostic. Its author reports writing the source before reading real bytes but publishing it after a preliminary outcome summary. Its `0.001`-nat diagnostic tolerance alone cannot settle the `0.0001`-nat ordering criterion; the full and second replays checked the recorded float32 path. These checks establish internal consistency of retained evidence within their stated scope, not independent model execution or independently attested inspection chronology. The maximum binary64-versus-saved-float32 selected-token diagnostic difference was approximately `2.28e-5` nat.
