# Annotation-only selection

The eight fixed same-site pairs were selected from the pinned annotation mirror
without a model forward. The manifest SHA-256 is
`6fc5159a57cf07a91e93c8bc38f27d3f5925877083532ce92fec828c6d777bfb`;
its preselection source is commit `20ba2249f8e385d1f81048ef27484c012afae93f`.
The retained selector receipt records exit 0, no timeout, and zero forwards.
The independent input audit reconstructed selection and verified the 19 unique
JPEG and processed-pixel hashes, including exclusion of every v1 image.
`auditor-sources.zip` retains the exact auditor and support-source bytes whose
SHA-256 values are recorded in the independent audit report.

The eight pairs have 16 distinct target images and three reusable non-target
donors. One cat donor is used in all eight pairs; the two dog donors appear in
five and three pairs. These observations are dependent. The pinned mirrors
and hashes establish byte consistency, not authenticated official COCO origin
or independence from model pretraining. The failed v2 spatial selector and its
zero-quadruplet result are retained separately.
