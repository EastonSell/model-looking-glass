# DistilGPT-2 agreement-head development selection

The fixed 5%-relative-dose development scan selected layer 3 head 4 and the second-ranked head 8 at the final-token input to attn.c_proj. Their mean opposite-versus-same donor contrasts were 0.1325635910 and 0.0098571777 logits respectively, across eight exposed targets. Ten heads qualified; heads 2 and 10 did not meet the prespecified dose eligibility rule and are not ranked. All 202 CPU forwards, the saved no-op/self/repeat controls, and two independent saved-byte replays agreed.

The 5% rule was chosen after inspecting clean activations from an earlier development attempt that stopped after 29 forwards when its much smaller global dose failed the float32 tolerance. Development is already exposed and the head selection is adaptive. Equal relative input doses do not imply equal absolute or projected doses; the runner-up is an adaptive active comparator, not a random-head null. These findings do not establish a held-out effect or a natural circuit.

selection.json freezes the candidate, comparator and full ranking before evaluation case construction. saved-records.zip contains the v1 failure and v2 source-bound event/terminal records and both model-free audit reports. It does **not** contain the 40,831,168 bytes of content-addressed tensor witnesses; those are retained in the local run directory, so this public package alone cannot replay the full tensor-level checks. The direct child was observed exiting zero and the outer launcher zero-exit observation was manually recorded; neither is an authenticated process-tree or execution attestation. No new evaluation input had been forwarded at selection.
