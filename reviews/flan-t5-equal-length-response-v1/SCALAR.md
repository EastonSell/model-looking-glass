# Independent scalar saved-evidence audit

`scalar_saved_audit.py` is a standard-library check of saved EOS-inclusive scores, rounded dose vectors, baseline gates, signed response curves and the six-pair decision. It binds the complete terminal, fixed case manifest, source checkout, committed blobs and public-observation record. It uses binary64 log-sum-exp to cross-check every saved float32 selected token log probability within a fixed `0.001`-nat diagnostic tolerance; it does not claim exact float32 log-softmax reproduction, full witness/landing verification or independent proof of model execution. The full separate auditor supplies exact f32 and witness checks.

The script was authored before its author opened or inspected the real result. Its source was published after the run had exited; the author had received a preliminary independent outcome summary before publication. Both fabricated fixtures passed before publication: 936 forwards with six positive pairs, and 648 forwards with two unevaluable, two negative and two positive pairs. The largest synthetic f64-vs-f32 selected-token difference was approximately `0.000113945` nat. Synthetic runs are not model evidence.

Production invocation requires `--run-dir`, `--cases`, `--terminal-sha256`, `--auditor-sha256`, `--source-checkout` and `--source-commit`. The auditor checks its own source bytes and the source checkout both before and after replay. The optional `--synthetic` switch is exclusively for fabricated fixtures and marks source verification false. No checkpoint or model is loaded by this audit.
