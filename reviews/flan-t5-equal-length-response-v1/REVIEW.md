# Independent equal-length dose-response runner checks

These checks exercise the fixed public CPU study's implementation on tokenizer-only inputs and fabricated model outputs. They do not load the FLAN-T5 weights, establish a real model result, or prove a future study's execution. The independent review is kept outside the implementation branch.

The locally reviewed implementation commit was `5db7543cb87cf8fc0c7737d74c8f217a9f186d0f`; its `looking_glass/flan_t5_equal_length_response.py` SHA-256 was `9b22ae62a338367d2423cce433a6235bce98637bf9c28ea71b44d300a7bb872c`. The scripts verify their requested commit and source bytes against Git. Use the exact corresponding published source commit when it becomes available, with its reviewed source digest.

- `no_forward_preflight.py` verifies the source, all seven offline asset hashes, selection plan, complete candidate screen, protocol and all 12 tokenizer rows; it does not load a model. Its fixed-shape evidence bound was 437,178,368 bytes under the 536,870,912-byte limit.
- `aggregate_oracle.py` checks all 729 combinations of six positive/negative/unevaluable pairs and 48 extra template-order/unsupported variants against a separately stated P/N rule. All 777 passed on the reviewed source.
- `full_fake_oracle.py` uses a fabricated CPU model and tokenizer while exercising the actual Looking Glass capture and patch path. The full fixture completed 936 forwards with 12 positive cells and terminal SHA-256 `d7a2807792bb8a16ce1095991990cae5a70e87acdf05ba7174af55dd9fc1f9b3`; the mixed fixture completed 648 forwards with two unsupported, two negative and two positive pairs, aggregate inconclusive, and terminal SHA-256 `5da310bfb0c2d28487703cef8b614869060a335967add8ef39083b3b6760a95f`. Both test processes exited zero. Their raw synthetic evidence was retained locally for separate auditor development, not represented as pretrained evidence.

The unchanged dose computation and f32 delivery path was also checked on the same tree with the separately published [`independent_runner_oracle.py`](https://github.com/EastonSell/model-looking-glass/blob/7fb2f25c4a579ac0848a12569101c7dea831280d/reviews/flan-t5-dose-response-v1/independent_runner_oracle.py): 100 seeded scalar trials, ten directional cell fixtures, a binary64 comparison boundary and ineligible/unmatchable cases passed. The earlier three-pair aggregate fixtures in that oracle test the reused earlier implementation; the six-pair aggregate uses `aggregate_oracle.py` above.

Run with a fresh empty bytecode prefix, CPU-only/offline variables and a dependency environment containing the pinned tokenizer and runtime. The fake script patches its model, tokenizer, publication and asset checks and must never be used to claim a real study run. The 777 fabricated aggregate cases test the operational rule, not statistical significance or generalization.
